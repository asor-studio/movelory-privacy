Privacy policy

How Movelory processes your data, during your journey and afterwards.

Data controller: robrosc
Privacy and support contact: asorstudio.contact@gmail.com
Effective date: 1 October 2026
Last revised: 21 October 2026

Local-first approach

Movelory does not require an account. It stores your profile, vehicles, journeys, GPS tracks, memories and videos in your device’s private storage. It does not include advertising, behavioural analytics, advertising profiling or remote crash reporting. Purchases and subscription status are processed separately through RevenueCat and the store.

Data processed on your device

  • Profile name and image;
  • Vehicle information;
  • Location, date and time of GPS points recorded during journeys, with accuracy, speed and heading when available;
  • Photos, videos, notes and other memories you select;
  • Recap videos generated locally;
  • A local diagnostic log limited to technical errors.

Data remains stored until you delete it from the app, clear app data through the operating system or uninstall Movelory.

Map services

When you open or query a map, Movelory makes HTTPS requests to:

  • Google Maps, to display map areas;
  • Google Places, to search for places using search text and geographic context when a Premium subscription is active.

These providers may receive your IP address and the technical data needed for the request. Movelory does not automatically send them your archive, photos, videos or complete GPS track. Third-party services process data under their own privacy policies and terms of use.

Journey distances are calculated locally from the recorded GPS track. No road routes are requested from external routing services, and no road distance between memories is estimated for journeys without a GPS track.

The example journey uses an illustrative track with static coordinates included in the app. Creating it does not request the device’s location. Displaying its map still uses Google Maps.

Latitude and longitude entered manually are processed locally and do not generate Google Places requests. Displaying the corresponding area still uses Google Maps.

Subscriptions

When the subscription service is configured, RevenueCat and Google Play or the Apple App Store verify purchases, renewals, expiry, refunds and restores. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous installation/customer identifier, transaction data and the technical information needed for verification. These checks may take place at startup, when returning to the app and while using Pro or Premium features. Movelory does not send photos, journeys, GPS tracks or coordinates to RevenueCat. Optional collection of advertising identifiers and optional SDK diagnostics are disabled.

Payments are handled by the store; Movelory does not collect payment card details. Restoring purchases uses the store account and does not require a Movelory account.

To apply the Pro quota, the device stores the subscription period start date and the number of stories created. This counter contains no names, photos or coordinates and is not sent to a Movelory server. Deleting the archive in the app does not reset the quota already used during the period.

Deleting local data does not delete transaction data retained by providers and does not cancel your subscription. Manage renewals through the store’s subscription settings. For requests concerning subscription data, use the privacy contact listed above.

Reference: RevenueCat privacy policy.

Permissions

Location is recorded only during a journey you start. On Android, an active journey uses a service with an ongoing notification to keep recording the route while the app is in the background, the screen is off or the app is removed from recent apps, subject to permissions and operating system restrictions. Recording and distance calculation take place on the device.

Pause suspends the saving of new track points; Finish stops recording. Changing screens does not cancel the pause. You can change or revoke location permissions in device settings. Without the necessary permissions, tracking is unavailable or interrupted.

The camera and microphone are activated only when you choose to create content. Notifications show the status of a journey or ongoing video generation.

During a journey, you can save a quick memory from the map or notification. Its location and time are stored locally so you can complete it later.

The optional floating button requires Android permission to display over other apps. It only provides a button to save a memory and does not read other apps’ content. You can disable it in Profile, in the quick memory menu or by holding it down. It disappears when the journey ends.

Diagnostics

Unexpected errors are recorded in a small local file. The file is not transmitted automatically. You may voluntarily export it or delete it in the “Privacy and data” section.

New diagnostic entries contain the date, technical error category and function names from the stack. Exception messages, URLs and file paths are not saved.

Your rights and contact details

Because there is no Movelory account or remote archive, you directly control deletion of local data. Exporting journeys and memories is not available in the current version; voluntary export of the diagnostic log is available. For privacy questions, support requests or to exercise your rights, contact the data controller robrosc at asorstudio.contact@gmail.com.

Where provided by applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw any consent without affecting previous processing. You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or the competent supervisory authority. Requests concerning data retained by providers will be handled according to their respective roles and obligations; deleting the local archive does not automatically delete transaction data.

Retention and deletion

The local archive remains until you delete it. The deletion command removes the profile, vehicles, journeys and their GPS tracks, memories, recaps and diagnostics managed by the app. It does not remove gallery originals, copies shared with other apps or data held by providers. Check device settings for any operating-system backups or copies.

The Pro quota stores the subscription period start and number of stories created locally, without names, photos or coordinates. This counter is not sent to a Movelory server and is not reset when the archive is deleted. Previous periods currently have no automatic expiry: their records remain until operating-system app data deletion or uninstallation, subject to any system restores.

The local diagnostic log is automatically reduced when it reaches approximately 256 KiB and can be deleted in the app. Rotation is based on size rather than a time limit.

Support communications are retained to handle and close the request; copies no longer needed must be deleted. Further retention is limited to information needed for legal obligations or a specific dispute and the applicable obligation or limitation period. Privacy requests and responses are retained to the extent needed to demonstrate how the request was handled.

Subscription data processed on the controller’s behalf is retained as necessary to verify and manage purchases, renewals, restores and disputes and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion through the privacy contact; data eligible for deletion and any justified retention will be assessed. Stores and other independent controllers also apply their own obligations and retention criteria.

Do not send identity documents in advance: further information will be requested only where necessary and proportionate to verify identity or locate data. You will normally receive a response within one month. Any extension permitted by GDPR will be notified within that month with reasons.

Do not send your entire diary for a request that does not require it.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. No solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects under Article 22 GDPR are made. Automated subscription checks enable purchased features; contact support about errors.
