Cookie information

This portal does not use cookies to operate or track your browsing.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This website

This information applies to the Movelory information portal, including its presentation, legal pages and demonstration videos. The privacy policy for the Movelory mobile app describes the app separately.

No cookies or tracking

The portal’s pages do not set cookies, including technical, analytics, advertising or profiling cookies. They do not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting or localStorage. Only when you close the banner with ×, a technical value (movelory.cookieNotice.dismissed.v1 = 1) is saved in sessionStorage to remember dismissal for the tab’s session, including navigation and reloads. The value does not identify you and is not sent to a server. Its lifetime follows the tab session managed by your browser, including any session restores. There is no visitor analytics or advertising integration. Menus and language links work without storing preferences on your device; choosing a language opens its corresponding page.

Images, fonts and videos

Images, fonts, scripts, styles and demonstration videos are served from the same site. Videos use the browser’s player, without embedded third-party players. The browser may cache downloaded files as part of its normal operation; this is distinct from setting cookies or profiling visitors.

Technical connection data

When you visit a published website, its hosting provider receives technical connection data needed to deliver pages and protect the service, such as your IP address, request date and time, requested resource and browser information. Not using cookies does not mean that no personal data is processed. Hosting logs and their retention depend on the hosting service and its applicable privacy information.

Links to other websites

External links, for example to Google or RevenueCat policies, take you to separate websites only when you choose to follow them. Those websites may use cookies or other technologies under their own policies. References to providers used by the mobile app do not mean their services are embedded in this portal.

Information and your choices

Because the portal does not use cookies or tracking technologies, it does not ask you to accept them and does not save a tracking consent preference. The × only means you wish to close the notice; dismissal is remembered for the tab’s session. The “Cookie info” tab remains available at the bottom to reopen the notice at any time. Reopening it does not clear the saved dismissal for other pages. If the browser blocks sessionStorage, the banner still closes on the current page but may reappear after navigation. The notice displayed on these pages is informational. If the portal introduces technologies requiring consent, this information and the related controls must be updated before those technologies are activated.
