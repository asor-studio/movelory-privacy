The service

Movelory lets you organise vehicles, journeys and memories, record routes and view them on a map. It is not an emergency service or a tool to rely on for safe navigation. Do not use the app in a way that distracts you while driving.

Free and Premium

Free includes one personal vehicle in addition to the example vehicle, up to five saved journeys per vehicle, seven memories per journey and three photos per memory. Videos do not count as photos. The example vehicle is read-only and does not allow new journeys to be created.

Premium adds place searches by name and removes these numerical limits. The example vehicle remains read-only. Maps, manual coordinates, tracking and recaps are also available in Free, subject to the applicable content limits. Exporting journeys and memories is not available in the current version and is not included in your purchase as an existing feature.

Monthly subscription

The applicable price, currency and billing frequency are shown in the app and on the Google Play confirmation screen before purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month unless cancelled through Google Play. Google Play handles payments and transactions; RevenueCat verifies access to Premium.

You can cancel renewal through Google Play subscription management, also accessible from the dedicated button in the app. Standard cancellation keeps Premium active until the end of the period already paid for. Uninstalling Movelory or deleting local data does not cancel renewal. Refunds and revocations may change the duration of access.

When the subscription expires, you return to Free. Previously saved content is not automatically deleted: if it exceeds Free limits, you cannot add more content beyond those limits without removing content or reactivating Premium. You can restore purchases using the Google Play account used to make them. Restoring your subscription does not restore deleted photos, journeys or other local data.

For refunds and help with charges, use Google Play procedures or contact support. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected, including applicable rights concerning withdrawal, conformity and remedies for digital services. These terms do not exclude or limit those rights.

Content and storage

You retain the rights to your content and are responsible for obtaining the necessary permissions for images, recordings and data concerning other people. Movelory stores your archive on your device and does not provide automatic cloud backup. Deleting data, uninstalling the app or losing the device may result in loss of the archive.

External services and support

Maps, search and route calculation depend on external services and connectivity; availability and accuracy may vary. Data processing is described in the privacy policy.
